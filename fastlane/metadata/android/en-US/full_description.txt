DocumentsUI Shortcut is a tiny launcher for Android's built-in Files / DocumentsUI app.

Tap the icon to open the system file manager in browser mode when supported by the device. Long-press the icon for quick shortcuts to Download, Documents, DCIM and Android/data, or add the Quick Settings tile.

It does not request any permission, does not read files, does not use the network, and does not keep a background service running.

Access to Android/data and Android/obb is controlled by Android and the device vendor's DocumentsUI implementation.
